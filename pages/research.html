Research Overview

Groups and societies arise from countless personal interactions.
How do constituent individuals understand these larger social patterns, and what advantages come from seeing the bigger picture?

The Challenge of Participation

From intimate dinner parties to packed concert venues, groups move and interact in ways that give rise to emergent patterns. These patterns may be percetible to uninvolved onlookers, but a question remains as to the extent individuals participating in these patterns are aware of them. How do individuals embed their own actions within the group's collective behavior? What social signals help us participate effectively in these different contexts?

I'm particularly interested in:

  • How interpersonally distributed patterns of bodily gestures, facial expressions, word patterns, and vocal intonations guide individual behavior in group conversations
  • Neural representations of groups and their emergent properties
  • How multimodal signatures in face-to-face interactions index the nature of relationships

Approach

Multi-Modal Recording

Capturing natural conversations and cultural events from multiple angles using synchronized audiovisual recordings.

Artificial Neural Networks

Using deep learning to extract subtle patterns in body language, speech, and group synchrony.

Dynamical Systems

Modeling social interactions as complex systems to understand how the pulse of social life emerges from individual actions.

Current Projects

Identifying collective social states in free-flowing group conversations

A group conversation may transition between boisterous laughter, heated debated, and juicy gossip all within the span of a few minutes. How do people use their communicative repertoires to orchestrate their collective navigation between these different states? What are the primary psychological dimensions that differentiate these states? How might different states serve different social functions?

To answer these questions, I've collected a dataset of group conversations by recording them with cameras at multiple angles and face-worn microphones. Using a collection of pre-trained deep neural networks, I've extracted the dynamic facial expressions, 3-dimensional body langauge, vocal intonation, and speech content of each participant in every conversation in addition to pre- and post-conversation surveys.
